Let a Duck use a computer

A Duck can get a computer of its own. Start it, watch the screen, and step in when it needs you.

7 stepsChecked against the app on 5 October 2026

Step1234567
  1. 1

    Step 1: See which Ducks have a computer

    Click Computers in the sidebar.

    Ducks are grouped under On and Off. No computer yet means the Duck hasn’t needed one. Off means its saved computer is stopped. A running one says whether it is working, idle, waiting for you or stopping.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Start a computer

    Click Start next to a Duck that is Off.

    It takes about a minute. Then its saved workspace appears, and you can watch or take control.

    Watch out. Start greyed out? Read the reason: computers may be paused for the company, or not allowed for that Duck. An owner or admin can change that in Controls. Starting also needs a connected AI.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Watch the screen

    Click the Duck’s name, then click Watch the screen.

    You see the live screen without taking over, and watching keeps the computer on. Go to screen means it is already waiting for you. To act yourself, click Take control on the screen; that pauses the Duck.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Hand the screen back

    Do what’s needed, then click Done, hand back to [Duck].

    While you have the screen, Paste text lets you put text or a password in it. In the browser view, Address bar, Tab and Enter send those keys. Close leaves the screen when you were only watching.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Stop a computer

    Click Stop when the computer should be off.

    Read the warning first: stopping disconnects anybody on the screen, and a waiting Duck will have to ask again. Stopping saves the workspace, but unsaved work on the screen can be lost.

    Good to know. Computers stop by themselves after a while idle. Having the list open doesn’t keep them on.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Choose which Ducks may use computers

    Click Controls, tick the Ducks under Ducks with computer access, then click Save controls.

    Allow computers for this company switches computers on or off for everyone. The window also shows the running limit, the idle time and the longest session.

    Watch out. Taking access away stops that Duck’s computer. These computers can change settings, install software and manage files.

    Good to know. Only owners and admins with company control can do this.

  7. 7

    Step 7: If something doesn’t work

    Find what you see below.

    • No Start button, or it’s greyed out: ask an owner or admin to allow company computers and add the Duck under Ducks with computer access. Teammates may also need Use the company computers on the Team page.
    • Desktop did not start: click Stop, then Start again.
    • The Duck asks you for a step: see Handle something that needs you.

That’s it.
