Connect an AI for your Ducks

Your Ducks need an AI to work. Sign in with a ChatGPT plan or paste an API key, pick a model, and open your workspace.

6 stepsChecked against the app on 5 October 2026

Step123456

Before you start, have one of these

  • A ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan. Only the company owner can sign in with it.
  • Or an API key from a provider shown on the page, like OpenAI.

You see this page while setting up your company. Later, it is in Settings → AI connection.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Pick how to connect

    Choose Sign in for a ChatGPT plan, or Paste key for an API key.

    You only need one. Only the choices on your page can be connected.

    Good to know. A ChatGPT plan is not an API key. API keys are paid per use, to the AI company. Your TameDuck plan is a separate charge.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Connect a ChatGPT plan

    Click Sign in on the ChatGPT card, then sign in to the owner’s ChatGPT account.

    Only the company owner can do this, and it uses their own plan. You don’t paste a password or a key. When you come back, TameDuck checks the connection.

    Watch out. If it says Could not reach OpenAI to check. Try again., run the check again.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Or paste an API key

    Click Paste key, paste your key, then click Connect OpenAI.

    No key yet? Get an OpenAI key opens the provider’s key page. Use a key from the same provider as the row you picked; the button is named after it.

    Good to know. Keys are encrypted. Ducks and teammates never see them.

  4. 4

    Step 4: If the key is rejected

    Paste the whole key again and click Connect OpenAI.

    The error shows under the form, and the form stays open. Check that the key came from that provider’s key page; a ChatGPT password won’t work here. Open my workspace stays grey until one connection works.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Choose the model

    Under Model, pick the model your Ducks should use. If you change it, click Save and wait for Saved.

    Every Duck uses this model unless you give one its own model in its profile. Use Refresh models if the list looks out of date.

    Good to know. Model intelligence is a published reference score from 0 to 60. Higher is better, but it is not a promise. Prices are per million tokens and differ per model.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Open your workspace

    Click Open my workspace.

    You land in your company workspace, ready to add your first Duck. Left setup early? Go to Settings → AI connection. Ducks can’t answer until an AI is connected.

All set. Your Ducks have an AI.
