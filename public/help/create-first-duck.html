Create your first Duck

Give your new teammate a name, a role, a face and a job, all in one window.

6 stepsChecked against the app on 5 October 2026

Step123456

Before you start

  • An AI connected to your company. Without one, your Duck can’t answer. Connect an AI
  • Permission to make new Ducks. Ask your company owner if you don’t have it.
  1. 1

    Step 1: Open Add a duck

    In the sidebar, click Add a duck under Direct messages.

    The Welcome a new duck window opens on Profile. Its tabs are Profile, Its job, Character and Notes.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Name it and pick a face

    Type a Name and a short Role in a few words, then click a face.

    Both a name and a role are needed. For more choices, click More faces and colours; Fewer faces and colours folds them away again.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Give it a job

    Click Its job and write what it looks after.

    Write it like a brief for a new colleague: what it looks after, what it may do on its own, and when it must ask you.

    Good to know. You can create a Duck with an empty job, but the window warns Its job is still empty. A clear job gives it direction.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Set its character

    Click Character and describe how it should behave.

    Its manner, what it cares about, and how it makes up its mind. This is optional: there may be starter text already, which you can change.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Add notes and check the model

    Click Notes and add what it should remember, like your preferences or decisions.

    The Duck adds to these notes as it learns, and everyone in your company can read them. Back on Profile, AI model says Same as the company. Click Change to give this Duck its own model.

    Watch out. If you pick a provider but no model, the window goes back to Profile and opens the model picker. Finish your choice there.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Create the Duck

    Click Create duck.

    The window closes. Your new Duck shows up under Direct messages, with its chat open.

    Watch out. If Name or Role is missing, the window goes back to Profile and marks it. Fill it in and click Create duck again.

Your first Duck is ready.
