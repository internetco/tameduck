Give a Duck its first task

Open your Duck’s chat, ask for one clear thing, and send it.

5 stepsChecked against the app on 5 October 2026

Step12345

Before you start

  • A Duck. No Duck yet? Create your first Duck
  • An AI connected to your company. Connect an AI
  1. 1

    Step 1: Open your Duck’s chat

    In the sidebar, click your Duck under Direct messages.

    Messages is the chat. Files and Tasks show its files and work, and Duck profile shows its role and job. The message box is at the bottom.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Ask for one clear thing

    Click the message box and say what you want, and what the result should be.

    For example a comparison, a draft or a short recommendation. Start with one focused task, so you can see whether it understood.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Add what it needs to know

    Add the files, notes, audience or deadline it needs, and say when it must stop and ask you.

    To attach something, click the paperclip, then A file from this computer or A company document. Up to 10 files per message, 25 MB each.

    For example

    “Compare the three product ideas in the notes above. Give me a short recommendation with reasons and links. Ask me if a source is missing; do not contact anyone.”

    Good to know. Ask it to check with you before it contacts anyone or spends money. Press Shift+Enter for a new line; on a phone, Return starts a new line.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Send it

    Click Send.

    Your message shows up in the chat, and the Duck starts working or replies when it can. Not quite right? Reply in the same chat with what to change.

  5. 5

    Step 5: If the Duck can’t start

    If the chat says Ready when you are, click Connect AI, connect an AI, and send your message again.

    It means no AI is connected yet. If an AI that worked before is down, a banner says who can fix it: an owner can try again in Settings → AI connection, and an admin can connect another one. Can’t manage AI connections? Ask your owner or admin.

    Good to know. If the Duck asks you a question, answer it in the chat so it can carry on.

Your Duck is on it.
