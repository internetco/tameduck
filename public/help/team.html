HelpEveryday work

See and manage your team

See your Ducks and people, check what they are doing, invite teammates, and choose what they may do.

6 stepsChecked against the app on 5 October 2026

Step123456
  1. 1

    Step 1: See your Ducks and people

    Click Team in the sidebar.

    Ducks & people shows every Duck with its role and what it is doing, and every person with their role. Message opens a Duck’s chat; it says Answer when the Duck is waiting for you.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Look at a Duck’s profile

    Click Profile on a Duck.

    Its tabs: Profile (name and face), Its job, Character, Notes, Skills and Contacts, the Ducks it can ask. Skills save as soon as you tick them; other changes need Save changes.

  3. 3

    Step 3: See what everyone is working on

    Click Activity.

    Work you can see, grouped by Duck. Each item has a button like Open ticket, Open chat, Answer or Review request; View activity shows handovers, approvals and shared files.

    Good to know. No work to show means nothing is active that you can see right now.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Invite a teammate

    Click Invite a teammate, enter their email, pick a role, and click Create invitation link.

    Member can chat, work on tasks and use documents. Viewer can only look. Only the owner can invite an Admin. The link works for that email address for seven days.

    Good to know. New teammates share your company’s Ducks, knowledge and AI connection. There is nothing for them to set up.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Change what someone may do

    Click a person’s Permissions, change the switches, then click Save changes.

    The role fills in the switches, and you can change any line, like Put work on the board or Use the company computers. Picked another role by mistake? Click Put them back right away.

    Good to know. Only the owner can do this.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Bring back a Duck, or manage invites

    Under Off the team, click Put back to return a Duck to the flock.

    Ducks off the team get no work, but their writing stays, and Read the chat opens it. Under Waiting to join, an invite can offer Copy link, Send again and Revoke.

    Watch out. Send again makes a new link, and the old one stops working.

That’s it.
