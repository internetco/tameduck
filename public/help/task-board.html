Track work on the task board

The task board shows all work as tickets. Find yours, add new ones, and set up boards with steps.

9 stepsChecked against the app on 5 October 2026

Step123456789
  1. 1

    Step 1: Pick a board

    Click Task board in the sidebar, then pick a board under TASK BOARDS.

    General has three columns: Open tasks, Being worked on and Done. Other boards have their own steps, left to right. The line under the board’s name says if work starts by itself, is paused, or waits for a person.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Find a ticket or your turn

    Type in Find a ticket…, or pick a Duck under All ducks.

    The count next to the filters updates as you go. When Your turn shows, click it to see tickets waiting for you. Show older Done tickets shows hidden finished ones; they were never deleted.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Open a ticket

    Click a ticket to see its brief, priority, who has it, its activity and files.

    On General, move a ticket between the columns as the work changes. On other boards, tickets move one step at a time, and can wait for a Duck, another step or an approval.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Finish your step or hand work back

    Write in Your note, then click Finish your step.

    A checker clicks Approve instead. Click a step at the top to read its instructions and work; Everything on this ticket shows its whole history.

    • Stuck? Write what went wrong and click Start a new attempt.
    • Send back returns the work to an earlier step. It needs a note.
    • Post note only adds a comment and moves nothing.

    Good to know. These need task permissions. Missing a button? Ask an owner to check your access on Team.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Add a ticket

    Click New ticket. Say what needs doing, add a Brief with what “done” looks like, and pick a Priority. Then click Create ticket.

    On General you can also pick an Assigned duck, or leave it for a person. New tickets start in the left column; on a board that runs by itself, its first Duck can start right away.

    Good to know. You need permission to edit tasks.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Make or change a board

    Click New board or Edit board, name it, and add its steps. Then click Save board.

    For each step, pick who does it (a Duck or a person), add instructions, and add a checker if someone must approve it.

    Good to know. Under More options, Ducks start their steps on their own and Tickets move on when a step is done switch the automatic parts on or off. Wait for other work first lets a step wait for chosen Ducks.

  7. 7

    Step 7: Archive or bring back a board

    In Edit board, click Archive board.

    Its tickets stay on it. To bring it back, open the board picker, click Archived boards, then Bring back.

    Watch out. If a Duck is still working on the board, let that finish first.

  8. 8

    Step 8: Change how the board looks

    Click Board view, pick the order and whether to hide old Done tickets, then click Save board view.

    Order can be Newest first, Oldest first, Recently updated or Priority (high first). This changes the board for everyone. Hidden tickets are not deleted.

  9. 9

    Step 9: See scheduled work

    Click Scheduled at the top of the board.

    It lists work set to run later. Click an item for its details. Don’t see Scheduled? Ask an owner to check your Team permissions.

That’s it.
